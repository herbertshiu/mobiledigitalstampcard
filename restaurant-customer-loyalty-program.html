Restaurant customer loyalty program that guests finish

The best restaurant loyalty programs are boring in the best way: easy to earn, easy to redeem, hard to argue about.

A restaurant customer loyalty program rewards return visits—stamps, points, or member perks—so regulars feel progress. Guests drop out when earning is slow at the till, rules change quietly, or redemption feels impossible.

Programme shapes that work

  • Visit-based stamps for cafés and QSR
  • Spend-based points for higher average checks
  • Member-only items or time-bound treats

Measure what matters

Track repeat visit rate and redemption—not just downloads. Related reading: restaurant CRM, customer loyalty program software, 會員 App 與促銷.
