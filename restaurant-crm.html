Restaurant CRM that fits a busy service

In restaurants, CRM only works if it respects ticket times and guest privacy.

A restaurant CRM stores guest history, preferences, and follow-ups so the team can re-engage regulars with relevant offers—birthday treats, win-back notes, or “almost enough points” nudges.

What good looks like on the floor

  • Capture that doesn’t stall the queue
  • Loyalty balances that match what staff say
  • Messages guests open because they’re useful
