Customer loyalty program software: buy for the guest journey

Feature lists sell software. Guests stay for clarity, speed, and fair rewards.

Customer loyalty program software typically covers enrolment, earning rules, redemptions, messaging, and basic reporting. For F&B and retail, the winning product is the one guests will actually use during a busy lunch rush.

Evaluation questions

  1. Can a guest earn in under a few seconds?
  2. Are rules understandable without a poster wall?
  3. Can you message with restraint (expiry, almost-there)—not spam?
  4. Do online and offline balances stay in sync?
