CRM software for small business Singapore

Small teams need CRM that takes minutes to learn—not a six-month implementation.

CRM software for small business Singapore should help you know who to care about next: first-timers, regulars, and guests who haven’t returned. Pair it with a simple loyalty mechanic so “relationship” isn’t only a spreadsheet.

Buyer’s shortlist

  • Works on phone and counter tablet
  • Clear consent and light data capture
  • Integrates with how you already take payments / orders
  • Reports that answer “who should we message?”
